Evidence / Sources

State Not Fate separates claim strength so public language stays honest while the project is still being developed and evaluated.

Evidence

Findings supported by established research, validated measures, or documented implementation results.

Inference

A practical conclusion drawn from evidence, design constraints, and observed behavior.

Hypothesis

A testable project idea that needs evaluation before becoming a public claim.

Opinion

A clearly labeled judgment or design preference, not a clinical claim.

Personal testimony

Individual experience that may be meaningful but should not be generalized as proof.

Public claim

Language intended for outside audiences; it must be supportable and conservative.
