## Core Principles (Stand-out Revelatory Connective Pieces)
- "State is information. Fate is a story we stop telling." (ANTI-GRAVITY 2.0). Current energy/mood/capacity/symptoms = data, never identity or permanent. Neuroplasticity via small repeated self-compassionate actions.
- Depression = multi-system assault (bio, psycho, social, behavioral, existential) that damages startup/initiation, reward registration, self-trust, future horizon. Not fixed by generic advice.
- Proof-based hope, not hype. Floor wins valid. Restart fidelity over compliance. "I restart without punishment."
- Energy sovereignty: 4 tiers (High / Medium / Low / Collapse) → MVD (Minimum Viable Day) + adaptive anchors/Polaris UI.
- Anchors + Mantras + Meditation + Circadian + Graded movement as load-bearing infrastructure.
- Stabilization before expansion. Ideal vs Realistic always distinguished.
- Human tone: blunt, precise, realistic, low-friction, no fluff/therapy/moralizing. Shared across PWA, prompts, and coaching.
- External memory & context engineering (AI Life Coach + trackers + status blocks + Obsidian/GitHub): "Context engineering beats clever prompting."
- AI as execution operator / Anti-Gravity Engine for the PWA.
- Fantasy boundary: Creative/roleplay (BE sources) strictly compartmentalized (GF MODE ON only).
- Professional care as primary: SNF is adjunctive self-management OS.
- Lived shaping: User's history (PRS cert, sober since 2019-11-01, job history, constraints of low money/energy/fog, fragile trust from instability/homelessness) demands low-friction, believable, restartable, non-moralizing design. 5-year vision: stable partnership + family in Cincinnati.
