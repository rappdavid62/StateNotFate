**Hub for the AI coaching, prompt, and execution layers that operationalize State Not Fate principles in daily life (job, routines, money, social, low energy, decisions).**

These systems share DNA with the SNF project: blunt execution operator, bottom-line first, no fluff/therapy/moralizing, **Ideal vs Realistic Move** given real constraints (energy, money, fog, transport), external memory via status/tracker files, proof/volume over perfection, one non-negotiable, restartable, low cognitive load.

## Core Systems
- **AI Life Coach Friend**: Full local Markdown OS (see full folder in Drive). Daily ritual with status block updates, templates, trackers (job, money, daily_log), social translator, emotional playbook, low energy plans. Master instructions enforce the response format and rules.
- **David AI Prompt Library**: 12 action-oriented commands (job search pipeline, apply, resume, council, research, low energy execution, social calibration, weekly review, tool decider, AI setup audit). Master context includes specific bio, certs (PRS APS.006470), job targets (field tech priority, HVAC, PRS, warehouse etc.), constraints, and work history.
- **ANTI-GRAVITY 2.0 + Grok Build prompts** (grok_build_polaris_master_prompt_v2.md in this repo): The PWA-specific coaching layer encoding full architecture, energy model, Polaris protocols, exact PHQ-9, 5-year vision, personal facts, and fantasy boundary.
