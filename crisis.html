Crisis / Urgent Support

State Not Fate is adjunctive support, not a replacement for professional care or emergency help.

If there is immediate danger

Call 911 or your local emergency number now, go to the nearest emergency department, or ask a trusted person nearby to stay with you while you contact urgent support.

If you are outside the U.S., use your local crisis line or emergency service. In the U.S. and Canada, you can call or text 988 for suicide and crisis support.
