## Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

The **State Not Fate Minimum Viable Dose (MVD)** framework is an evidence-grounded, friction-calibrated intervention system designed for states of acute functional disruption, severe depressive paralysis, neurodivergent executive burnout (ADHD/Autism), and suicidal distress.

In severe depression, the brain's internal **cost-calculator** (mesolimbic dopaminergic valuation) is mechanically warped: ordinary tasks feel astronomically expensive, while prospective reward is registered as zero. Standard wellness advice ("exercise for 30 minutes", "cook a healthy meal", "think positive") demands cognitive and metabolic resources that are offline during a system crash.

An **Anchor** is not a habit, a chore, or a self-improvement goal. An Anchor is a **low-cost biological or somatic circuit-breaker** designed to survive collapse days, preserve the physical floor, and provide external evidence that an altered state is not a permanent fate.

## Level 0: Severe Lethargy & Bedbound Floor Anchors (0% – 2% Battery)

When rising from bed is physically impossible and motor retardation or catatonic heaviness dominates.

### 0.1 The Supine 4-Ounce Hydration Sip
- **Execution Time**: 15 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Keep a pre-positioned water bottle within arm's reach of the pillow. Take two deliberate swallows without sitting upright.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Swallowing activates cranial nerves IX (Glossopharyngeal) and X (Vagus), triggering an immediate parasympathetic tactile signal and breaking dry-mouth sensory numbness.
- **Clinical Rationale**: Rehydrates the vascular bed after overnight dehydration, subtly improving cerebral perfusion without demanding orthostatic adjustment.

### 0.2 The 120-Second Daylight Window Gaze
- **Execution Time**: 120 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Turn head toward the window or crack open blinds from bed. Keep eyes open toward ambient sky light for two minutes.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Intrinsically photosensitive retinal ganglion cells (ipRGCs) expressing melanopsin transmit photic signals directly via the retinohypothalamic tract to the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN), suppressing daytime melatonin leakage.
- **Clinical Rationale**: Bypasses the requirement to get dressed or step outside while still providing the essential zeitgeber (time-giver) to prevent circadian phase drift.

### 0.3 Proprioceptive Heavy Pressure Anchor
- **Execution Time**: 60 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Pull a heavy duvet, folded quilt, or weighted blanket firmly across the torso and thighs. Place hands flat across the lower ribs.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Deep Touch Pressure (DTP) stimulates tactile and proprioceptive mechanoreceptors, downregulating sympathetic arousal and elevating oxytocin/endorphin tone.
- **Clinical Rationale**: Halts the subjective sensation of physical fragmentation and motor dread during severe anxiety-depression paralysis.

### 0.4 The 10-Second Cervical / Pelvic Micro-Shift
- **Execution Time**: 10 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Slowly rotate ankles three times, or gently roll head side-to-side on the pillow.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Vestibular and spindle afferents fire back to the cerebellum and motor cortex, breaking functional akinesia (motor freezing).
- **Clinical Rationale**: Confirms motor agency to the nervous system with virtually zero metabolic caloric burn.

### 0.5 The Biological State-Truce
- **Execution Time**: 30 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Speak or whisper the factual script: *"My nervous system is in an acute biological low-power brownout. This is a physiological shutdown, not a moral failure. I declare a temporary operational truce."*
- **Biological Mechanism**: Prefrontal verbalization downregulates hyperactive amygdalar catastrophizing and interrupts the acute dorsal raphe self-blame cascade.

## Level 1: Autonomic & Somatic Regulation Anchors (2% – 10% Battery)

Designed for rapid somatic state shifts when overwhelmed by vegetative numbness or trembling agitation.

### 1.1 The Mammalian Dive Reflex / Facial Cold Pressor
- **Execution Time**: 30 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 2 / 10
- **Action**: Submerge face in a bowl of cold tap water (or press a cold, wet cloth or ice pack over eyes and cheekbones) while holding breath for 10–15 seconds.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Stimulation of the ophthalmic and maxillary branches of the trigeminal nerve triggers the mammalian dive reflex: instantaneous vagally mediated bradycardia (heart rate reduction) and peripheral vasoconstriction.
- **Clinical Rationale**: One of the fastest non-pharmacological methods to clamp acute sympathetic tachycardia and terminate runaway panic loops.

### 1.2 The Physiological Sigh (Double Inhale + Extended Exhale)
- **Execution Time**: 60 seconds (3 consecutive rounds)
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Inhale deeply through the nose, immediately top off with a second sharp micro-sniff of air, then release a long, slow sigh through mouth until lungs are empty.
- **Biological Mechanism**: The second inhale re-inflates collapsed pulmonary alveoli, increasing surface area for carbon dioxide offloading. The extended exhale increases intrathoracic pressure, slowing sinoatrial node firing.
- **Clinical Rationale**: Rapid autonomic reset backed by neurobiology (Huberman, Spiegel et al., *Cell Reports Medicine* 2023).

### 1.3 Bilateral Somatic Butterfly Tapping
- **Execution Time**: 60 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Cross arms over chest with fingertips resting just below the collarbones. Alternate tapping left and right in a steady, slow rhythm (1 tap per second).
- **Biological Mechanism**: Alternating bilateral somatosensory stimulation engages inter-hemispheric communication, downregulating amygdalar hyperarousal (EMDR somatic analog).
- **Clinical Rationale**: High-utility anchor for acute emotional flooding and uncontainable somatic dread.

### 1.4 Palmar Cooling / Cold Can Grounding
- **Execution Time**: 45 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Hold an ice-cold beverage can or ice cube firmly in the palms of both hands.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Glabrous (hairless) skin on the palms contains high concentrations of arteriovenous anastomoses (AVAs), providing direct thermal feedback to the hypothalamus and preoptic area.
- **Clinical Rationale**: Immediate sensory redirect that grounds attention in concrete temperature physics rather than internal rumination.

## Level 2: Neurodivergent & Executive Dysfunction Micro-Initiation (ADHD / Autism / Brain Fog)

When working memory deficits, dopamine baseline depletion, and initiation paralysis block all forward momentum.

### 2.1 The 30-Second Single-Object Touch
- **Execution Time**: 30 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 2 / 10
- **Action**: Walk up and physically touch ONE item required for a task (e.g., touch running shoes, touch the toothbrush, touch the laptop lid). Do not commit to doing the task.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Lowers initiation friction below the perceived energetic cost threshold. Separates physical approach from cognitive task execution.
- **Clinical Rationale**: Defeats ADHD task avoidance by removing prospective planning demands.

### 2.2 Pre-Positioned Visual Staging Anchor
- **Execution Time**: 45 seconds (night before or during a clarity window)
- **Physical Friction**: 2 / 10
- **Action**: Place tomorrow's medication, water glass, or clothing in the direct visual line-of-sight where the person's feet touch the floor.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Replaces internal working memory retrieval with external perceptual cues (environmental affordance).
- **Clinical Rationale**: Executive dysfunction brains require salient visual prompts to trigger action scripts without cognitive negotiation.

### 2.3 Binary Micro-Fork (Elimination of Multi-Choice Paralysis)
- **Execution Time**: 15 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: When staring at multiple tasks, force a binary choice: *"Only Choice A (stand up) or Choice B (drink water). Nothing else exists in the universe."*
- **Biological Mechanism**: Relieves prefrontal striatal selection fatigue. In severe depressive states, choice complexity scales linearly with paralysis.

### 2.4 Passive Body Doubling / Audio Tether
- **Execution Time**: Ongoing
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Turn on a pre-recorded State Not Fate companion audio or low-tempo ambient track while performing one minimal physical movement.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Co-regulatory auditory anchoring provides low-level sensory novelty, stabilizing baseline dopamine without cognitive strain.

## Level 3: Circadian & Chronobiology Anchors

Grounded in suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN) science, triple chronotherapy, and melatonin regulation.

### 3.1 The 3–5 Minute Photic Lock
- **Execution Time**: 3 to 5 minutes
- **Physical Friction**: 3 / 10
- **Action**: Step outside into open sky within 30–60 minutes of waking. Look in the general direction of the sun without staring directly at it. No sunglasses.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Delivers 10,000 to 100,000+ lux to retinal ganglion cells, setting the circadian clock's phase response curve, optimizing the Cortisol Awakening Response (CAR), and timing melatonin onset ~14–16 hours later.
- **Clinical Rationale**: Central biological intervention for delayed sleep phase disorder, seasonal affective disorder, and adolescent circadian uncoupling.

### 3.2 The Weekend Wake-Time Floor
- **Execution Time**: Zero ongoing effort (clock rule)
- **Physical Friction**: 3 / 10
- **Action**: Maintain wake time within a 60-minute window 7 days a week, even after poor sleep. Do not sleep in 4 hours on weekends.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Prevents "social jetlag", which creates acute metabolic and mood destabilization equivalent to crossing 3 time zones every Monday morning.

### 3.3 The 90-Minute Evening Photic Dimming
- **Execution Time**: 30 seconds to switch lights
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: 90 minutes before targeted sleep, switch off high-lux overhead lights. Rely exclusively on low-level floor lamps or amber/red hues.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Eliminates 460–480nm wavelength blue light that suppresses pineal melatonin secretion.

### 3.4 The 10-Minute NSDR (Non-Sleep Deep Rest) System Reset
- **Execution Time**: 10 minutes
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Lie supine, close eyes, and listen to a structured guided body scan / NSDR audio track when mid-day energy collapses.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Shifts cortical EEG activity from high-frequency beta waves to alpha and theta waves, restoring striatal dopamine reserves without causing sleep inertia.

## Level 4: Cognitive Fog & Rumination Interruption Anchors

Designed to uncouple emotional distress from toxic cognitive loops and self-blame.

### 4.1 The External Loop Dump
- **Execution Time**: 90 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 2 / 10
- **Action**: Take a physical scrap of paper and pen. Scribble out the exact 3 repetitive thoughts currently circling the mind. Fold the paper and set a heavy object on top.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Offloads working memory burden from the frontoparietal control network to physical external storage. Breaks the default mode network (DMN) rumination cycle.

### 4.2 State-Not-Fate Cognitive Reframe Script
- **Execution Time**: 20 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Mentally repeat: *"My thoughts right now are symptoms, not prophecies. An exhausted brain predicts eternal exhaustion. I do not take life evaluations from a dying battery."*
- **Clinical Rationale**: Separates current biological state from existential identity, preventing temporary pain from hardening into permanent fatalism.

### 4.3 The Micro-Proof Registration (The Floor Win Log)
- **Execution Time**: 30 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: After performing any minimal anchor (swallowing water, looking at daylight), record one checkmark or note in the State Not Fate app or notebook.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Generates a discrete Response-Contingent Positive Reinforcement (RCPR) signal. Re-sensitizes blunted mesolimbic dopamine prediction error circuits.

## Level 5: High-Arousal Crisis & Means Safety Anchors

*Mandatory Safety Gate: If in acute danger, immediately call or text 988 (U.S.) or text HOME to 741741.*

### 5.1 Lethal Means Time-Delay Lock
- **Execution Time**: 60 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 2 / 10
- **Action**: Separate and store medications, sharp objects, or firearms behind a multi-step lock, high shelf, or with a trusted person.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Acute suicidal crises are characterized by profound temporal myopia and impulsivity (frequently lasting under 60 minutes). Introducing physical friction creates a vital window for ambivalence to re-emerge.
- **Clinical Rationale**: Means restriction is the single most empirically validated suicide prevention intervention in public health literature.

### 5.2 The 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Shock Grounding
- **Execution Time**: 90 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Name out loud: 5 things seen, 4 things physically felt, 3 distinct sounds, 2 distinct smells, 1 physical taste.
- **Biological Mechanism**: Forces primary sensory cortices to take metabolic precedence over hippocampal/amygdalar threat simulation.

### 5.3 The 988 One-Tap Bridge
- **Execution Time**: 10 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Tap the 988 shortcut on the phone or State Not Fate app. Send the single letter: *"Hi"*.
- **Clinical Rationale**: Lowers communication threshold during acute verbal paralysis.

## Level 6: Low-Friction Social Micro-Anchors

To prevent catastrophic social isolation without the exhausting overhead of social performance.

### 6.1 The Zero-Obligation Thumbs-Up
- **Execution Time**: 10 seconds
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Send a single thumbs-up emoji `👍` to a pre-designated support contact.
- **Pre-Agreed Contract**: *"If I send a thumbs up, it means I am alive and breathing, but do not have the battery to text or speak. No reply is needed."*
- **Clinical Rationale**: Eliminates the guilt-avoidance-ghosting spiral that destroys social networks during depressive episodes.

### 6.2 The Low-Bandwidth Status Script
- **Execution Time**: 15 seconds (copy-paste)
- **Physical Friction**: 1 / 10
- **Action**: Send: *"Hey, going through an acute low-energy state today. Off-grid resting. Safe. Will check back in a couple days."*
- **Clinical Rationale**: Preserves relationships while honoring biological recovery limits.
